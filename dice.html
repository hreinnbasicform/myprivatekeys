Gear and process

Casino-style dice, if you gather entropy by hand.

Physical dice are one way people collect randomness away from a keyboard. A set of five is a common starting pack for base-6 workflows. People often buy two packs — ten dice — so each roll covers more ground. Buying dice does not create a key, and this page will not turn faces into words.

How to use the dice

This is process, not a recipe that produces a seed on this site.

  • Use casino-style dice with clear faces. A 5-pack is a usual start; two packs (10 dice) make each throw cover more ground.
  • Roll in a private room. Treat the faces as secret numbers, the same way you would treat a seed.
  • Record each face carefully on paper. If you mis-copy a face, you have a different secret than the one you rolled.
  • Do not photograph rolls into a phone camera roll or cloud album. A picture of dice is a picture of entropy.
  • Do not type the faces, sums, or “dice codes” into a website — including this one.

Converting rolls to BIP39 — offline only

Dice give you raw randomness. Turning that randomness into a BIP39 phrase is a mapping step. That mapping must happen on an offline device or other air-gapped workflow you already trust.

myprivatekeys.com will not do it. There is no converter here, no wordlist picker, and no button that prints words. If a page in a browser offers to turn your rolls into a seed, close it.

The Bitcoin / BIP39 page covers why 12 and 24 words differ in size, and why you write the finished phrase on paper — never into a website.

One air-gapped example: SeedSigner

SeedSigner is an open-source, air-gapped Bitcoin signing project people build from off-the-shelf parts. Among other things, the official project describes creating a BIP39 seed from D6 dice rolls on the device itself — the rolls never need to go through a browser.

This is education, not an endorsement and not an affiliate link. Other offline tools exist. Use what you have already decided to trust, after your own review.

  • Verify firmware and downloads from the official project (seedsigner.com), not from a random mirror or a chat-file.
  • Prefer air-gapped use: the usual SeedSigner story is a device without Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth, talking to a wallet with QR codes.
  • Never enter a seed into a browser, a phone “checker” app, or any site that offers to validate the words.

This site still will not generate a phrase. SeedSigner (or any other offline tool) lives on hardware you control. We only point at the official project so you know the name and the usual URL.

What not to use

Skip random websites, browser extensions, and online “dice to seed” tools. They can see the rolls, invent words you did not choose, or both. A convenient tab is not an air gap.

What we will not do

We will not invent other product claims, publish a roll-to-word converter, or accept a photo of dice “to generate a seed.” If you use dice as part of a BIP39 process, the mapping happens offline on tools you already trust. Never type the resulting words into this website — or any other.

Entropy size is still the story on the home page: 12 words ≈ 2128 ≈ 3.4×1038; 24 words ≈ 2256 ≈ 1.2×1077; twenty-four is about 2128 times larger than twelve, not twice as large.
