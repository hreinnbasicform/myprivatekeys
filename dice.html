Casino-style dice, if you gather entropy by hand.

Physical dice are one way people collect randomness away from a keyboard. A set of five is a common starting pack for base-6 workflows. People often buy two packs — ten dice — so each roll covers more ground. Buying dice does not create a key, and this page will not turn faces into words.

What we will not do

We will not invent other product claims, publish a roll-to-word converter, or accept a photo of dice “to generate a seed.” If you use dice as part of a BIP39 process, the mapping happens offline on tools you already trust. Never type the resulting words into this website — or any other.

Entropy size is still the story on the home page: 12 words ≈ 2128 ≈ 3.4×1038; 24 words ≈ 2256 ≈ 1.2×1077; twenty-four is about 2128 times larger than twelve, not twice as large.
